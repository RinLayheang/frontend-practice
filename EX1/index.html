My First Blog Post: Visiting Angkor Wat

Last holiday I went to Siem Reap with my family to visit Angkor Wat. We woke up at 4am to see the sunrise behind the temple, and honestly it was worth it. The sky turned orange and pink and the reflection on the water in front of the temple was really beautiful. There were a lot of tourists but everyone was quiet when the sun came up.

After the sunrise we walked inside the temple. The walls have very long carvings that tell stories, like the Churning of the Ocean of Milk. I was surprised how detailed they are, because they were made almost 900 years ago without any modern tools. If you want to know more about the history, you can read the Angkor Wat article on Wikipedia.

Conclusion

Visiting Angkor Wat was one of the best trips I had. It made me proud of Cambodian history and I want to go back again to see the other temples like Bayon and Ta Prohm. If you never went there, I really recommend it, just remember to bring water and wake up early!
