Who we are

SlideSpace was started in 2024 by a small group of students in Phnom Penh. We were tired of slide tools that are slow and expensive, so we decided to build our own.

  1. 2024 - the idea started in a classroom
  2. 2025 - first version online with 100 users
  3. 2026 - more than 10,000 people use SlideSpace
